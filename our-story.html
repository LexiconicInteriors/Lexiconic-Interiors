Our Story

Lexi

Hi, I’m Lexi! I live in Wilmington, NC with my wonderful husband and son. I have always been interested in storytelling and capturing the moment. That passion led me to pursue a degree in film and, after graduation, a career spanning filmmaking, film festival programming, photography, and work in the film industry. Later, I transitioned into administration, event planning, and project management, where I discovered just how much I love bringing ideas to life and making all the moving pieces work together.

As I began envisioning a family and the kind of home I wanted to create, my dreams shifted. I realized that beautiful, functional spaces could set the stage for some of the most important stories of all: the stories of our lives! That realization led me to interior design, where I earned my interior design diploma through The Interior Design Institute.

I love helping others bring their dreams to life by designing environments that enhance their lifestyles. My approach goes beyond styles and trends; it’s about creating spaces that feel good to you.

I believe that thoughtful design has the power to bring peace, comfort, excitement, and joy into our lives. Let’s have fun and make things beautiful together!

Justin

Every great space tells a story. Over the past 18 years as a Creative Director and visual artist, I’ve had a career that’s taken me from an imaginative childhood in the Catskill Mountains to creating high-profile work for global brands. Along the way, I’ve learned so much about how visual elements shape the way we experience the world. I’ve always believed that a space has the power to transform us. It’s the silent narrator of our lives, setting the emotional tone for every moment, relationship, and memory that unfolds within it.

That fascination with how spaces shape our stories is what led me to my newest venture with my best friend and creative partner, Lexi. Since meeting 11 years ago, we’ve built a wonderful creative connection that shapes everything we do. Together, we love getting to know our clients and blending our different perspectives to create custom spaces that don’t just look beautiful, but truly inspire the people who inhabit them.
