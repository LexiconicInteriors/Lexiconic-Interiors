Featured Projects

Wandering Cone Creamery

We worked closely with the shop’s owner to transform a blank slate into her vision for a welcoming, travel-inspired ice cream shop. From space planning and mood boards to material selection, lighting, signage, and custom details, we helped turn her ideas into a cohesive space that reflected the Wandering Cone brand. It became a fun collaboration between the owner’s vision, her friends’ and family’s DIY talents, and our design and project management expertise, resulting in a sweet, joyful space.

Beachy Bright Escape - V1

This room started as a true “vanilla box”. Beige walls, beige carpet, and broken blinds. The vision was to create a warm, playful, beachy retreat that felt fresh and inviting without feeling overly babyish. Every detail was thoughtfully chosen with the future in mind, creating a space that could evolve alongside the child rather than needing a complete makeover in a few years. A little imagination, a little color, and a whole lot of heart turned this blank space into a room full of possibility.

An Organized Start

A place for everything, and everything in its place. This custom wardrobe was designed around the things it needed to hold, creating storage that works with the way the space is actually used. But beautiful storage is only part of the equation. Thoughtful organization and easy-to-maintain systems are what make it functional long-term. The result is a wardrobe that feels beautiful, effortless, and easy to keep that way.

Beachy Bright Escape - V2

As the nursery’s little one grew, the room grew too. The next chapter called for a fun, functional big-kids room while keeping the beloved beachy theme. A playful boat bed and palm tree lamp brought even more personality to the coastal-inspired space, while the former changing-table wall became a dedicated art / invention station. The result is a room that feels a little more grown-up while still capturing the imagination and playfulness of childhood.

Sunny the RV

This cosmetic transformation was inspired by the desire to take a little piece of home on the road. The original interior was heavy and brown, lacking the brightness, playfulness, and sense of adventure that makes RV travel so much fun. The reimagined interior has a more cheerful, spirited personality that better reflects its purpose: recreation, exploration, and making memories. Now, the journey itself feels just as fun and exciting as the destination.
